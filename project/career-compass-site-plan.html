01

Site Name

Career Compass

Represents guidance and direction for people building a career in technology — just as a compass helps travelers find the correct path, the site helps students, graduates, and career changers navigate the opportunities available in tech.

Optional domain: careercompass.tech

02

Site Purpose

Career Compass is an informational website designed to help students, recent graduates, and career changers explore careers in the technology industry. It provides detailed information about tech career paths — including Software Development, Data Analytics, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, UI/UX Design, Artificial Intelligence, and DevOps — covering what each career involves, the skills required, recommended certifications, and beginner-friendly learning roadmaps.

The goal is to help visitors confidently choose a technology career path and understand the steps needed to begin.

03

Target Audience

  • High school graduates
  • University students
  • Recent graduates
  • Career changers
  • Self-taught learners
  • Anyone interested in transitioning into technology
04

Site Goals

  • Help visitors confidently choose a technology career path and understand the steps needed to begin.
  • Give each career path a clear, honest picture of the skills, roadmap, and certifications it actually requires.
  • Build visitor trust through testimonials and a "Why Choose Tech" section that reinforces the site's credibility before visitors commit to a path.
  • Grow into a portfolio-quality resource that continues to help people beyond this course.
05

User Personas

Z

Zainab, 18

High School Graduate

Just finished secondary school and is curious about tech but has no coding background. She wants a low-pressure way to see which careers exist and which one might suit her before committing to a program.

T

Tunde, 29

Career Changer

Currently in a non-technical role and looking to move into tech. He needs a realistic roadmap — skills to learn, certifications worth the time and money, and how long the transition typically takes.

E

Efe, 22

Self-Taught Learner

Has been learning on and off from scattered YouTube videos and articles. She wants a single, trustworthy hub that organizes free and paid resources by career path instead of by random topic.

06

Scenarios

  • I have no technical background. Which technology career would be the easiest for me to start?
  • What certifications should I earn if I want to become a Data Analyst?
  • What is the difference between Software Engineering and Cybersecurity?
  • Which learning resources are free, and where should I begin?
  • I'm not ready to commit yet — how do I stay updated on new career paths and resources without creating an account?
07

SEO Plan

  • Target career-intent keywords per page (e.g. "how to become a data analyst," "cybersecurity certifications for beginners," "cloud computing roadmap").
  • Write a unique meta description and descriptive alt text for every career profile and image.
  • Link Career Explorer profiles to related Learning Hub resources so pages reinforce each other internally.
  • Submit a sitemap to Google Search Console once the site is live.
